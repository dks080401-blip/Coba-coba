Our Love Story

Our story began on an ordinary autumn afternoon in New York City. Charlotte was reaching for the last book on a little bookstore shelf when Alexander, standing just beside her, reached for the very same one. A brief laugh turned into a conversation, and a simple afternoon became the beginning of something neither of them had expected. What followed were countless coffee dates, long walks through the city, spontaneous weekend trips, and quiet evenings that somehow became their favorite memories. Over time, they discovered that love wasn’t always about grand gestures. Sometimes, it was about finding someone who made an ordinary Tuesday feel extraordinary. And then, on a beautiful autumn evening overlooking the Connecticut countryside, Alexander asked Charlotte the easiest question she had ever answered. She said yes. Now, surrounded by the people who mean the most to them, they are ready to begin their next chapter. And so, their forever begins.

FAQ

For any questions regarding the celebration, please contact our wedding planning team.

What time should I arrive?

We kindly recommend arriving at least 20–30 minutes before the ceremony begins to allow time for seating.

Is there a dress code?

Yes! Our dress code is Black Tie Optional. Please refer to the Dress Code section for further details.

Will transportation be provided?

A complimentary shuttle service will operate between selected hotels and the wedding venue. Please check the Transportation section for the schedule.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones. However, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration, with the exception of those specifically named on the invitation.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Due to limited seating, we kindly ask that only guests named on the invitation attend.

What if I have dietary requirements?

Please include any dietary requirements in your RSVP so that we can accommodate you as best we can.
